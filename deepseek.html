DeepSeek V4 Visualizer

HCA, CSA, Sliding Window & Lightning Indexer — a hybrid attention architecture enabling 1M-token context with near-linear scaling.

DeepSeek V4 interleaves two complementary attention types: CSA (Compressed Sparse Attention) with 4:1 compression and top-k selection via the Lightning Indexer, and HCA (Heavily Compressed Attention) with 128:1 compression for cheap global context. A local sliding window in CSA layers preserves immediate context precision.
CSA: 4:1 compress → Lightning top-k → sparse attn
HCA: 128:1 compress → dense attn (cheap global)
Lightning Indexer: FP4 ReLU dot-product scorer

HCA — Heavily Compressed Attention
128:1 compression. Dense attention over ultra-compressed entries — acts as a cheap global "memory" layer. Retains broad summaries of entire context.
Ultra-Compressed Blocks (128:1 simulated)
Lightning Indexer — FP4 Relevance Scoring
Lightweight ReLU-activated dot-product in low precision (FP4). Rapidly scores all compressed blocks and selects top-k most relevant for full attention computation. The key to near-linear O(N·k) complexity.

Method Properties

✅
KV Cache: 90% memory reduction via dense chunking (CSA/HCA compression).
✅
Quadratic Scaling: Lightning Indexer enables sparse top-k selection — near-linear O(N·k).
✅
Immediate Context: Uncompressed sliding window in CSA keeps recent words exact.
✅
Long-Term Amnesia: Nothing permanently deleted. HCA retains broad summaries with retrievable CSA details.
❌
Lost in Middle: Synthesizing deeply buried facts from compressed blocks still degrades reasoning accuracy.
