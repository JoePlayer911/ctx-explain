StreamingLLM Visualizer

"Efficient Streaming Language Models with Attention Sinks" — Discovers that initial tokens act as attention sinks, and keeping them enables stable infinite-length streaming.

Standard sliding-window eviction causes perplexity explosion once the sequence exceeds cache size, because the model loses its "anchor" tokens. StreamingLLM fixes this by always retaining the first few "sink" tokens (typically 4) alongside the recent sliding window — no fine-tuning required.

Method Properties

✅
KV Cache: Permanently drops oldest non-sink tokens to keep cache static.
✅
Quadratic Scaling: Fixed window caps exact-match comparisons.
✅
Immediate Context: Sliding window preserves recent context perfectly.
❌
Long-Term Amnesia: Once a token falls out of the window, it's permanently forgotten.
❌
Lost in Middle: Only remembers beginning sinks + recent end. Middle is abandoned.
